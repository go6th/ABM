# Independent review: deliverable files and web UI (7e0ad095 .. working tree)

Reviewer scope: `src/exports/` (workbooks, Word, zip), `web/` archive and sheet views, against `docs/parity/reference_spec.md` and `reference_manifest.json`. Read-only; scratch scripts and renders are under the session scratchpad. Pytest was not run. `npx tsc --noEmit` passes; vitest on sheet-view, tab-view, archive-pages, render, server-api and server-pages passes (34 tests).

Evidence method: fresh render of the synthetic corpus (`render_samples.py`), adversarial builds through `ExportBuilder` with fake ports (hostile titles, 40,000 character descriptions, odd URLs, empty account, brand set, 7,000 postings), LibreOffice recalculation of every workbook, `python -I` + openpyxl / python-docx / unzip on outputs and on the owner's reference files, a vitest/jsdom harness and Playwright screenshots (390 and 1280 px) of the web components.
